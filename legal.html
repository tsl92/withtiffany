When you open it, Vercel's servers process technical data your browser sends automatically (IP address, date and time, page requested, browser and device type) to deliver the site securely. It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device; visits are counted in aggregate (page, referring site, country, browser and device type) and cannot be traced back to you. Legal basis: legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR / UK GDPR).

I use this only to answer your enquiry and, if we work together, for that work.
